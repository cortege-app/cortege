# Numbers
- 15: SAMU, medical emergency.
- 17: police emergency.
- 18: fire brigade.
- 112: European emergency number.
- 114: emergency number for deaf or hard-of-hearing people, by text message, video call, chat or fax.

These calls are free and available 24 hours a day, from a landline or a mobile, even if the phone is locked or has no credit.

Source: service-public.gouv.fr and gendarmerie.interieur.gouv.fr, checked on 18 October 2026.

# The call
- 15 for a health problem: feeling unwell, serious injury, unconscious person.
- 18 for an accident, a fire, rescuing a person.
- 17 for an offence: assault, violence.
- 112: single number, usable for any of these needs.

Give the person who answers:
- the phone number you are calling from,
- your name if needed,
- the nature of the problem: feeling unwell, illness or accident,
- any risks,
- the exact location,
- the number of people involved,
- the condition of each victim,
- what you have already done.

Then answer the questions from the emergency services or the doctor.
